A note on the sound, science & your privacy

Sound, not a prescription.

Stillwave blends evolving chords, soft notes, a bass bed and optional generated noise. A sine-shaped loudness pulse affects the low–mid music bus, primarily 200–1000 Hz; bass and atmosphere stay separate. Modes are listening suggestions, not proven brain states.

Focus starts at 16 Hz: 16 pulses per beat at 60 BPM (64th-note subdivisions). Only this default Focus rate is explicitly aligned to that musical grid. Other Focus rates and the Relax, Sleep and Meditate presets use experimental independent modulation, not a rate-derived musical clock. Relax starts at 8 Hz, sleep at 1 Hz and meditate at 6 Hz. You can change any rate or turn modulation off. This is not Brain.fm, is not clinically validated and does not claim the same effects.

Local by design.

Audio is synthesized on your device, not downloaded. No accounts, analytics, tracking, microphones or paid APIs. Preferences are stored locally; your hosting provider may log normal page requests.

Your first web visit needs internet. Use Save offline and check for “Ready offline” before disconnecting. Browser storage can be cleared or evicted. The Android APK bundles the app and generator, including on its first offline run.

Listen gently.

Start at a low volume. Headphones are optional. Stop if uncomfortable; avoid listening where attention to your surroundings matters. For a sleep session, set a timer and keep volume low.

Web browsers may pause audio with the screen locked or in the background. The Android app uses its native playback service instead. Stillwave is original software and branding; it does not include Brain.fm audio or proprietary technology.

On the web

  1. Open Stillwave online once.
  2. Choose Save offline and wait for Ready offline.
  3. In Chrome or Edge, use Install app in the browser menu. On iPhone or iPad, use Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Installation is optional. Offline storage may be cleared by your browser; you can recheck it at any time. App updates keep your saved preferences.

On Android

The APK is bundled: the first launch can be offline. Start a session in the app to use native service playback. Your device’s battery settings can still affect background playback.

Controls

Start, pause, resume or stop with the player. Keyboard users: Tab to any control, Enter or Space to activate buttons, arrow keys to adjust sliders. Stop resets the timer; pause preserves it. New sound starts a fresh session with a new seed.
