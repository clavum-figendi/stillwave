Field notes, research & credits05 / READ OFFLINE

Evidence, with limits.

Woods, Sampaio, James, Przysinda, Cordovez, Hewett, Spencer, Morillon and Loui (2024), Rapid modulation in music supports attention in listeners with attentional difficulties, Communications Biology 7, 1376, DOI 10.1038/s42003-024-07026-3, studied amplitude-modulated music, task performance and neural responses. Effects varied with experimental order and self-reported attentional difficulties. A pulse rate is not a universal attention aid. [1]

The study confined added modulation to a 200 Hz–1 kHz band with controlled musical content and spectral balance. Stillwave instead modulates a generated music bus, with separate bass and atmosphere routing. It is not a reproduction of those band-limited stimuli. No study of Stillwave establishes benefits for focus, ADHD, sleep or meditation.

The corrected disclosure identifies Kevin J. P. Woods as a Brain.fm employee with unexercised equity options, and Adam Hewett as an employee and equity holder when the research was conducted. [5] A separate correction added Bernardo Cordovez to the author list. [6] This evidence should not be presented as independent validation of this app.

A pulse is a control, not a promise.

Focus starts at 16 Hz: 16 pulses per beat at 60 BPM (64th-note subdivisions). Only that default is explicitly aligned to the musical grid. Other rates and modes use experimental independent modulation, not a rate-derived musical clock. Relax starts at 8 Hz, Sleep at 1 Hz and Meditate at 6 Hz. These names are listening suggestions, not validated brain states.

Stillwave uses amplitude modulation: changes in loudness, not binaural beats. Headphones are optional for operation; stereo width zero gives mono output. This does not claim equivalent cognitive effects through speakers and headphones. The web waveform shows software output, not loudspeaker pressure or brain activity. The native modulation sketch is illustrative, not an output measurement.

Start quietly. If the pulse distracts you, set Modulation depth to 0%. More depth is not necessarily better. Compare settings at a similarly comfortable perceived loudness; equal slider positions need not sound equally loud. Hearing risk depends on loudness and exposure time. WHO recommends keeping volume down and taking breaks. [2] App percentages cannot establish safe decibels without calibrated device/headphone measurements. Stop if uncomfortable; never mask surroundings where awareness matters.

Local by design.

Preferences stay in local browser/WebView storage. No analytics, microphone use or runtime audio uploads. Your hosting provider may log ordinary website requests. Reset sound and Reset display only change their respective settings.

The first web visit needs internet. Choose Save offline and wait for “Ready offline” before disconnecting. Browser storage can be evicted or cleared. The Android APK bundles the interface and native generator, even for its first offline launch.

Browsers may suspend background audio or playback with the screen locked. Android uses its native playback service; manufacturer battery management can still interfere.

At the workbench

All sounds are generated locally. No login or audio downloads.

Getting started

Choose a mode, turn the volume low and press Start session. Style changes the arrangement; Synth voice changes the timbre. Open Layer mixer to balance music, bass and melody. Atmosphere adds a synthesized texture, not a recording.

Saving for offline listening

  1. Open Stillwave online once.
  2. Choose Save offline and wait for Ready offline.
  3. Optionally use Chrome or Edge’s browser menu to install. On iPhone or iPad: Safari → Share → Add to Home Screen.

Installation is optional; browser storage may be cleared. Recheck offline readiness before travelling. Updates keep the same saved preferences.

Android

The APK is bundled: its first launch can be offline. Native service playback handles the audio; battery settings may affect background playback. External source links are blocked in the app. Read or copy their displayed URLs and open them manually in a separate browser.

Playback & keyboard

Pause preserves your place; Stop resets the timer. New sound chooses a seed and resets the session. Tab moves between controls. Enter or Space activates buttons; arrow keys adjust sliders. Presets set style, pulse rate and depth, not a medical outcome. Depth zero turns loudness modulation off.

Listen gently

Headphones are optional: this is amplitude modulation, not binaural beats. Use low volume. Stop if uncomfortable and avoid use where attention to your surroundings matters.
