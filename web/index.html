How it works

  • Composed live, never looped. Chords, melodies and rhythms are written on your device as you listen.
  • Optional pulse. A soft loudness modulation on the music. Turn it off in Settings › Advanced.
  • Private and offline. No account, stream or tracking. Save it once to listen anywhere.
  • Listen gently. Start quiet and take breaks. This is a listening aid, not a treatment.
Research, limits & credits

Woods et al. (2024), Rapid modulation in music supports attention in listeners with attentional difficulties, Communications Biology 7, 1376, studied amplitude-modulated music. Effects varied with listening order and self-reported attentional difficulty; it does not show that one pulse rate helps everyone.1 Its stimuli confined modulation to 200 Hz–1 kHz; Stillwave modulates its whole music bus, so it does not reproduce those stimuli. Focus 16 Hz is a starting point; other rates are experimental independent modulation.

A 2026 correction discloses that Kevin J. P. Woods is a Brain.fm employee with unexercised equity options, and Adam Hewett was an employee holding equity during the research.5 A 2025 correction added Bernardo Cordovez as an author.6 Stillwave is not affiliated with or endorsed by Brain.fm and makes no claims about focus, ADHD, sleep or meditation outcomes.

Hearing risk depends on loudness and time; WHO advises keeping volume down and taking breaks.2 Atmospheres are synthesized textures, not recordings.
